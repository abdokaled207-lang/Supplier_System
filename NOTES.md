# Notes on User's World

## Domain & Business
- **Business**: Roti Chani Supplier System (supplier operations in Melaka/Johor, Malaysia).
- **Core Entities**: Customers, Products, Orders, Stock Receipts, Payments, Reports.
- **Key Channels**: WhatsApp / WhatsApp Business (for customer orders and invoices).

## Terminology
- **Loop**: A recurring pattern in life, week, morning, or repeated activity.
- **Workflow**: The spec of one loop, made real (`workflows/*.md`).
- **Trigger**: What fires each run (event or schedule).
- **Checkpoint**: Human-in-the-loop point to verify or decide.
- **Push right**: Defer checkpoint as far as possible; do maximal work before involving human.
- **Brief**: Tight, decision-ready summary presented at a checkpoint.
